Route Overview

Route (DC)Paradise TH (5,400 ft) → Muir Snowfield → Camp Muir (10,188 ft — rope up here) → Cathedral Gap (10,880 ft) → Ingraham Flats (11,100 ft) → DC entry / cleaver toe (~11,300 ft) → Disappointment Cleaver spine (rock/mixed; rockfall zone) → top of DC (~12,300 ft) → upper Ingraham/Emmons → ID/DC junction (~13,000 ft, verify) → summit plateau → Columbia Crest (14,411 ft)
DescentSame route reversed. Record GPS ascent track and use on descent. Descend the cleaver before afternoon sun loosens rock.
Bail / turnaroundReverse route from any point; Camp Muir is the refuge. Stay roped on the glacier; manage rock/mixed down-climbing carefully on the cleaver.
Key hazards (1) Cleaver rockfall — the DC is a rock rib; rock loosens as the sun hits it. Descend before ~10–11 AM; helmets from Muir; tight spacing, no stopping in the fall line.
(2) Firm / hard refrozen snow & névé/ice — this week's high winds scoured the upper mountain and cooling restored a hard refreeze. Confident cramponing and self-arrest required; steel crampons (see Surface note).
(3) Wind slab on lee (E–SE) aspects from strong NW winds Jun 16 — assess directly.
(4) Crevasses on the Ingraham/Emmons above the cleaver — roped, probe bridges.
(5) Moderate WNW wind 20–23 mph + wind chill ~10–14°F — full insulation.
(6) Serac / icefall on the Ingraham flank — move continuously through exposure.
Reassessment pointsCamp Muir · Ingraham Flats (cleaver conditions, wind) · top of DC (time + surface) · ID/DC junction (time gate) · Summit (30-min cap)

Field Abort Criteria

  • Any person calls stop or requests turnaround — immediate compliance, no debate
  • Unstable wind slab (cracking, whumpfing, hollow feel) on lee aspects — retreat / reroute
  • Unmanageable rockfall on the cleaver — wait, detour, or turn around
  • Sustained wind >35 mph at team's elevation; any precipitation; cloud base below team
  • Any crevasse fall, even self-rescued — reassess before continuing
  • AMS symptoms above 10,000 ft (headache + nausea, ataxia, behavioral change) — descend
  • Missed gate = turn around: Muir 12:30 AM · junction 5:30 AM · hard turnaround 7:30 AM
