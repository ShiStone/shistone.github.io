Avalanche: No NWAC daily forecast in July; dry-slab risk negligible (no recent storm/wind-loading). Do not carry a beacon/shovel/probe set. The only snow-instability concern is minor wet-loose sluffing on sun-warmed slopes later in the day — handled by early timing. This is a non-glaciated walk-up: no rope, no crevasse gear.

Route Overview

Item Details
Primary route Cold Springs TH (~5,600 ft) → South Climb trail (Trail 183) to Lunch Counter ~9,000 ft → snow slopes to False Summit ~11,500 ft → loose summit plateau to true summit 12,281 ft
Descent Reverse the ascent line (record the track up). Glissade the chutes where snow is continuous and rock-free; scree-walk the exposed sections (~7,500–7,900 ft) and the dry trail below ~7,200 ft
Bail / turnaround route Descend the ascent route. From anywhere, retreat to Lunch Counter and out.
Key hazards Firm-snow slip; exposed rock in glissade chute; loose summit scree; cold/wind
Reassessment points Lunch Counter (go/no-go for the upper snow + hard-turnaround clock); False Summit (final go/no-go)

Route Timing

Pace method: strong single-push pace (calibrated to your Rainier c2c ~19 h on much harder terrain) at an easy, no-sweat effort on this non-technical walk-up — ~1,150 ft/hr trail→snow, ~1,050 ft/hr on the upper snow slope, slow on the loose summit plateau. Light day pack. Fixed adds: crampon transition, water, breaks. A midnight start puts you back at the car ~1.5 h before the noon deadline.

Food / Water / Waste Plan

Water

Adams has limited reliable liquid water — unlike a glacier route with meltwater refills, plan to carry it from the car. The cool, cold-morning conditions keep the sweat rate down, so the full hot-day 5–6 L is not needed here.

Item Plan
Starting water ~3.5–4 L from the car (cold night start = lower sweat than a hot day, but a long climb + reflective snow + your higher-than-average intake)
Refill point(s) Optional: filter from snowmelt streams/runnels below ~7,200 ft if flowing early season; do not count on it. No reliable running water high.
Cache (optional) If you'd rather not carry it all up, cache ~1 L at Lunch Counter (~9,000 ft) on the way up and grab it on descent — climb the upper slope lighter. Mark it (bright bottle / GPS point).
Electrolytes Carry salt/electrolyte tabs — your intake runs high; balance plain water to avoid hyponatremia.
Stove for melt Optional/leave it for the single push; snow above ~7,200 ft can be melted in an emergency but it's slow.

Field Abort Criteria

  • Firm ice steeper/harder than comfortable for self-arrest → turn around.
  • Not at Lunch Counter by ~03:30 (reassess) or hard turnaround 08:00 wherever you are.
  • Glissade chute rock-choked / uncontrollable → scree-walk down, no glissade.
  • Deteriorating weather or wind chill beyond your layers → descend.
